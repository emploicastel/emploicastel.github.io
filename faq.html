Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about this demo website and how it can be adapted for real travel services.

No. Prices shown in this demo are sample presentation content and should be replaced with verified live offers before publishing.

Not yet. The forms are front-end demos only and are not connected to a booking engine or payment processor.
Does emploicastel guarantee flight or hotel availability?＋
No availability claim is made in this demo. Real availability should only be displayed after connecting verified travel providers.
Can the website support custom trip requests?＋
Yes. The included trip planner form can later be connected to your email, CRM, database or booking workflow.
Can I change the destinations and packages?＋
Yes. All content is static and editable in the HTML files.
